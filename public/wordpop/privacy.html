Word Pop Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Word Pop ("the app") is a word search game published by Casey Battle. This policy explains what information is collected when you play and how it is used.

Information the app itself stores

The app has no accounts and no sign-up. Your progress (level, coins, hints, stars, daily gift streak and sound setting) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us, and deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, which is provided by Google LLC. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • approximate location based on your IP address,
  • device and app information (such as device model, operating system version and app version), and
  • ad interaction data (such as ad views and taps).

This data is handled by Google according to its policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • If you are in a region that requires consent (such as the EEA, UK or Switzerland), the app asks for your ad consent on first launch. You can change it at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings under Google > Ads.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new date.
